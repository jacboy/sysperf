Benchmark your Linux server with one command.

Disk I/O, latency and download speed to 34 locations on six continents, and CPU performance. Open source, cleans up after itself, uploads nothing.

$curl -fsSL https://getsysperf.com/bench | bash $wget -qO- https://getsysperf.com/bench | bash
  • Debian 10+ · Ubuntu 18.04+
  • x86_64 and ARM
  • About 8–10 minutes
  • Version __VERSION__

What it measures

System

CPU model, cores and frequency, RAM, swap, disk size, OS, kernel, virtualization type, ISP and ASN, and whether IPv6 works.

Disk

Three 30-second fio runs: random read/write, 512 KB blocks, queue depth 64, direct I/O. Measures the disk of the directory you run it from.

Network

Latency to 12 cities and download speed from 34 hosts of 8 providers, grouped by continent, plus the nearest CDN node. 100 MB or 15 seconds per host.

CPU

sysbench on one thread and on all threads. Optionally Geekbench 7 as well.

Options

Add Geekbench 7 to the CPU test by putting GEEKBENCH=7 in front of bash, not in front of curl:

$curl -fsSL https://getsysperf.com/bench | GEEKBENCH=7 bash
  • A 462 MB download, verified against a pinned SHA-256 checksum before it runs. Needs about 1.5 GB free disk and 2 GB RAM, otherwise it's skipped.
  • The free Geekbench version uploads its result and basic system information to the public Geekbench Browser. Without GEEKBENCH=7, nothing is uploaded anywhere.
  • Adds 5–10 minutes to the run.

Before you run it

Piping a script into bash means trusting it. The script is short, readable and open source. You can download and read it first:

$curl -fsSLO https://getsysperf.com/bench && less bench && bash bench

What it does to your system

  • Installs missing packages from your distribution with apt-get, after asking: curl, wget, jq, fio, ping, sysbench. Root is only needed for that.
  • Writes temporary files to a private .sysperf.* directory in the current directory and removes it at the end, also on Ctrl+C.
  • If the download breaks off, nothing runs: the script only starts on its last line.

Who it talks to

  • ipinfo.io for your ISP and ASN, and ipv6.icanhazip.com for the IPv6 check, over HTTPS.
  • The latency and speed-test hosts listed in the README.
  • cdn.geekbench.com and browser.geekbench.com, only with GEEKBENCH=7.
  • Nobody else. sysperf collects no data, and this site uses no cookies, analytics or third-party resources.
